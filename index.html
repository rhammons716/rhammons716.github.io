Kernel

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own PC. It plans his week from his calendar, tasks and mail.

What it does

  • Plans the week and keeps the calendar free of conflicts.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Gmail to do that.
  • Adds or moves only its own calendar events and tasks.
  • Sends the owner one weekly summary email.

Kernel is a private app used by one person. It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for or log in to here.
